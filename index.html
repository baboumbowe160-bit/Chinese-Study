About the voices

Voice 1 is a native speaker who recorded each whole word in one go. It sounds natural, the way people really speak, including the light syllables (the second 谢 in 谢谢, 么 in 什么). Use this voice most of the time.

Voice 2 is a different native speaker who recorded every syllable on its own. The app joins her syllables to make words, so it can sound a little separated, but each tone is very clear. Use it when you are not sure about a tone.

Voice 2 has no light (neutral-tone) syllables, so for words like 谢谢, 什么 or 朋友 the app plays Voice 1 instead and tells you.

Recordings: Voice 1 – Yue Tan (Shtooka / audio-cmn), Voice 2, the chants and the sound exercises – Chen Wang (audio-cmn, github.com/hugolpz/audio-cmn). Shared under the CC BY-SA licence.

Phone voice is your phone's own reading voice. It can read any word, including words you add yourself. You can choose a male or female voice for it in your phone settings (see below).

Sound not working?
  1. Turn up the media volume. Press the volume button while the app is open, and check the phone is not on silent or Do Not Disturb.
  2. Open the app in Chrome, not inside WhatsApp, Facebook or another app. Those built-in browsers often block sound. Tap ⋮ → Open in Chrome, or install the app from Chrome.
  3. Tap Listen again. The very first sound sometimes needs a second tap.
  4. First time offline? Open the app once with internet so the recordings are saved. After that they work offline.
  5. Bluetooth: if headphones or a speaker are connected, the sound goes there.
  6. Close and reopen the app to make sure you have the newest version.

Phone voice silent? Your phone needs its Chinese voice:
Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech → Preferred engine: Google → tap ⚙️ → Install voice data → Chinese (Mandarin) → download.
To pick a male or female voice, tap the Chinese voice there and choose one. On some phones the menu is Settings → General management → Language → Text-to-speech.

If nothing works, try Voice 1 or Voice 2. They are recordings and do not need the phone's voice.
